== Description ==

Pedicure Makeup Store is a modern, luxurious, and multipurpose theme designed for beauty and wellness businesses, nail salons, pedicure and manicure services, makeup studios, spa centers, cosmetology clinics, skincare specialists, beauty consultants, hair care professionals, nail technicians, wellness entrepreneurs, facial treatment providers, and aesthetics studios looking to elevate their online presence. Featuring a clean, minimal, responsive, and retina-ready design, this theme ensures a visually stunning and seamless user experience across all devices. Built with secure, optimized code and fast-loading pages, it supports smooth navigation and enhanced performance. Pedicure Makeup Store includes customizable banners, animated sections, strategically placed Call to Action (CTA) buttons, product galleries, dedicated testimonial and team sections, and social media integration to boost engagement, credibility, and conversions. Integration with the Contact Form 7 plugin enables easy client inquiries, appointment bookings, and customer communication. With Bootstrap-based layouts, interactive shortcodes, rich personalization options, and translation-ready functionality, the theme allows full creative control to match brand identity while supporting agency workflows and global reach. Perfect for launching a spa, beauty salon, pedicure service, or makeup store, Pedicure Makeup Store combines elegant design, interactive elements, and professional functionality to deliver a secure, scalable, and visually captivating online platform that attracts and retains clients.

= Installation =
	
1. In your admin panel, go to Appearance -> Themes and click the 'Add New' button.
2. Type in pedicure-makeup-store in the search form and press the 'Enter' key on your keyboard.
3. Click on 'Install' button to install and 'Activate' button to use.
